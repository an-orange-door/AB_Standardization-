# Papers to request through USC

For Jordan Halsey. DOIs are Crossref-verified. Grouped by what each would
actually change — **nothing in `building_plans.md` paraphrases a paper listed

## Request these four first

**1. Park, K., Ergan, S. & Feng, C. (2024).** "Quality assessment of residential
layout designs generated by relational Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs)."
*Automation in Construction* **158**, 105243.
DOI **10.1016/j.autcon.2023.105243**

> The most on-point paper nobody could read. An empirical audit of
> House-GAN-lineage output against architect-made plans — 80,000+ each side —
> tested on space-size distribution, visibility/privacy and room connectivity.
> It is the evidence for *how* learned plan generation falls short, which is the
> whole basis for the "generate with rules, train nothing" recommendation.

**2. Solihin, W. & Eastman, C. (2015).** "Classification of rules for automated
BIM rule checking development." *Automation in Construction* **53**, 69–82.
DOI **10.1016/j.autcon.2015.03.003**
⚠ The widely-cited DOI ending `2015.01.003` is **wrong and 404s** — use the one above.

> A four-class taxonomy of rule complexity. The single most useful item for
> deciding **what is machine-checkable at all**, which gates the entire egress /
> code-checking effort.

**3. Koźmiński, K. & Kinnen, E. (1985).** "Rectangular duals of planar graphs."
*Networks* **15**(2), 145–157. DOI **10.1002/net.3230150202**

> The rectangular-dual theorem itself — turning a room-adjacency graph into a
> plan. Only free restatements were read, and a later paper claims a
> counterexample to its separable case, so the original matters.

**4. Bhasker, J. & Sahni, S. (1988).** "A linear algorithm to find a rectangular
dual of a planar triangulated graph." *Algorithmica* **3**, 247–278.
DOI **10.1007/BF01762117**

> The O(n) construction that makes (3) practical. The free conference-version PDF
> exists but would not text-extract.
