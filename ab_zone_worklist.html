AssetBash Zone Coverage

Every latest-version AB tool, measured by cooking it and reading s@name. Zone coverage gates the material system — without zones, AB::MaterialBinding has nothing to key off and no Principled branch can be generated. Class decides which contract applies: a Processor emitting no zones is correct, not a defect.

Classes are suggestions — shown dashed until you confirm or change one. They come from explicit name lists, not pattern matching: a loose regex classified ResortGenerator as a helper because “Resort” contains “sort”. “Did not cook” means unmeasured, not broken — those tools were instanced with no input, and processors legitimately need upstream geometry. Packing keys are excluded from all counts: piece0-style names are unique per piece, not shading zones.
